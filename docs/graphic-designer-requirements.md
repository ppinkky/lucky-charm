# Graphic Designer Requirements

Track the visual production work in [Graphic designer checklist: Thai fortune ritual visual assets](https://github.com/ppinkky/lucky-charm/issues/8).

## Scope

Own the visual language, restaurant logo treatment, bowl illustration states, fortune-slip artwork, typography, color system, motion reference, social artwork, and production-ready exports. Use the [UX/UI requirements](ux-ui-requirements.md) for flow and interaction behavior.

## Required visual direction

Create a tactile, magical, playful visual language connected to the Thai restaurant and food context. Keep it legible in a busy restaurant environment. Avoid generic fantasy, financial prediction, medical, or authoritative religious-divination styling.

## Bowl states

Provide art-directed states for intact/ready, wobble or energy build, pressure pause, cracked, open/releasing, and reset. The crack and slip emergence should read as one physical magical moment. Include a 2-4 second storyboard or motion reference and a reduced-motion treatment.

## Fortune-slip system

Provide one shared responsive frame for all 12 records. Accommodate the fortune number, name, overall prediction, love, wealth, work and study, health, optional lucky dish, restaurant logo, English view, and Thai view. Test the longest English and Thai entries without unreadably small type.

Thai may be revealed by swipe or a clearly labeled `ภาษาไทย` control. Do not invent fortune text or interpretation.

## Typography

Document display, body, UI, and Thai typefaces; glyph support; weights; licenses; web permissions; English and Thai fallbacks; minimum sizes; line height; spacing; long-name rules; mixed-language behavior; and numeral style.

## Color and contrast

Document primary, secondary, accent, background, slip surface, text, muted, focus, completion, error, and crack/reveal colors. Provide HEX, RGB, and HSL or equivalent tokens, usage guidance, WCAG contrast checks, and restaurant-lighting notes. Never use color as the only state or language indicator.

## Exports and social artwork

Provide editable source files, optimized SVG where appropriate, transparent PNG fallbacks, logo exports, state-specific bowl assets, fortune-slip frame, per-fortune art, background/decorative assets, loading/failure artwork where applicable, favicon/metadata artwork, and a complete asset inventory.

Each asset needs a filename, format, dimensions or viewBox, intended display size, focal point, crop-safe area, background variants, and animation state mapping. Provide a 1080 x 1920 portrait share composition using the visible fortune and restaurant logo only. Test the longest English and Thai examples.
