# Fortune Ritual Designer Requirements

## Purpose

Create the visual system and production-ready assets for a mobile-first fortune-telling art experience made with a Thai restaurant in New York.

The visitor holds a private question, begins a short bowl ritual, receives one of 12 prepared fortune records, and can share the visible fortune. The experience should feel tactile, magical, playful, and connected to the restaurant without claiming guaranteed prediction.

This document is the visual handoff checklist. The product decisions are fixed unless explicitly marked as a designer decision.

The checklist is split into two focused GitHub issues:

- [UX/UI designer checklist: Thai fortune ritual experience](https://github.com/ppinkky/lucky-charm/issues/9)
- [Graphic designer checklist: Thai fortune ritual visual assets](https://github.com/ppinkky/lucky-charm/issues/8)

Use the UX/UI issue for interaction, screen flow, responsive behavior, and accessibility. Use the graphic designer issue for visual direction, assets, typography, colors, illustration states, and exports. The original combined issue 7 is superseded.

## Fixed Product Decisions

- Primary audience: restaurant visitors using a phone, usually from a QR code or NFC tag.
- Mobile is the primary surface. Desktop must remain usable with click interaction.
- English is the primary interface and fortune language.
- Thai is available through `ไทย` or `ภาษาไทย` controls and through a swipe between the English and Thai fortune views.
- The visitor does not type a question. Nothing about the question is sent, stored, or displayed.
- Every attempt randomly selects one record from all 12 prepared fortune records.
- A fortune record contains:
  - Fortune number
  - Fortune name
  - Overall prediction
  - Love
  - Wealth
  - Work and study
  - Health
  - Lucky dish, when applicable
- The result is a fortune slip, not a new interpretation generated by the website.
- The ritual sequence is: bowl interaction, increasing wobble, pause under pressure, visible crack, bowl opening, slip emergence, and final settlement.
- The full animated sequence should take approximately 2-4 seconds.
- The bowl returns to its intact state for every new attempt.
- Device shake is optional. Tap is always available. Desktop uses click.
- Sound is optional and starts muted.
- Reduced-motion users receive a short direct transition to the result.
- The result remains visible after sharing or downloading.
- No account, analytics, persistent fortune history, or direct social-platform publishing integration is required.

## Experience Screens

### 1. Opening screen

Required content:

- Restaurant logo
- English-primary opening copy:
  - `Think of a question.`
  - `Hold it quietly in your mind. Nothing is typed or saved.`
  - `Begin`
- Compact Thai language control: `ไทย`
- Quiet framing, such as: `For reflection, fun, and a little luck.`

The screen should make the bowl ritual feel immediately available. Do not add a login, form, questionnaire, onboarding carousel, or required text input.

### 2. Bowl ritual screen

Required content and states:

- Intact fortune bowl
- Primary action: `Shake the bowl (or tap the bowl instead)`
- Optional motion-permission prompt:
  - `Let the bowl feel your shake?`
  - `You can also tap the bowl instead.`
  - `Enable shake`
  - `Tap instead`
- Sound control, starting muted
- Tap/click fallback visible or immediately understandable
- Loading state that does not leave the bowl inert
- Recoverable asset failure state:
  - `The bowl needs a moment. Try again.`
- Motion fallback state:
  - `Shake unavailable. Tap the bowl instead.`

Required bowl artwork states:

1. Intact and ready
2. Wobble or energy build
3. Pressure pause
4. Cracked
5. Open or releasing the slip
6. Reset to intact

The artwork should support the suspenseful pressure-release choreography with playful physical wobble. The crack must feel art-directed, not like an arbitrary digital crop.

### 3. Fortune result screen

Required content:

- Fortune number
- Fortune name
- English fortune content as the primary view
- Restaurant logo
- `ภาษาไทย` control or link
- Swipe affordance between English and Thai views where practical
- `Share fortune`
- `Download image`
- `Try again`

Required result behavior:

- Thai is the same fortune record, not a second draw.
- The currently visible language is the language used for the share image.
- The result must remain readable on a small phone screen.
- Do not force all long English and Thai content into one crowded face.
- Do not invent extra categories, claims, or interpretations.

### 4. Failure and accessibility states

Design visible states for:

- Assets loading
- Asset load failure
- Motion permission available
- Motion permission denied
- Device without a motion sensor
- Tap fallback
- Reduced motion
- Sound muted and sound enabled
- Keyboard focus
- Screen-reader announcement state
- Retry after failure

The implementation will announce these moments concisely:

- `The bowl is shaking.`
- `The bowl is cracking.`
- `Your fortune is ready.`

## Visual Direction

The visual direction is owned by the designer and restaurant/art-project collaborators. Establish a distinctive visual language that feels:

- Tactile and physical
- Magical without becoming generic fantasy
- Playful but not childish
- Connected to the Thai restaurant and food context
- Legible in a busy restaurant environment
- Strong enough to work in a QR-code-first mobile experience

Avoid making the interface look like a financial prediction service, a medical service, or an authoritative religious divination system.

### Color system deliverables

Define and document:

- Primary brand color
- Secondary brand color
- Accent or action color
- Background colors
- Fortune-slip surface color
- Primary text color
- Secondary text color
- Muted or disabled color
- Focus-visible color
- Success or completion color, if needed
- Error or recovery color
- Crack/reveal highlight color
- Contrast-safe alternatives for small text and controls

For every color provide:

- Name and purpose
- HEX value
- RGB value
- HSL or equivalent design-token value
- Light/dark usage guidance if applicable
- WCAG contrast checks for text, controls, and focus indicators
- Print or restaurant-lighting notes if the artwork may be printed

Do not rely on color alone to communicate language, state, error, focus, or completion.

### Typography deliverables

Define:

- Display typeface for the fortune name or key moment
- Body typeface for fortune content
- UI typeface or a clear rule for reusing the body typeface
- Thai typeface with matching visual character and complete required glyph support
- Font weights and styles actually needed
- License and web-use permissions for every font
- Fallback stack for English and Thai
- Minimum readable sizes for mobile and desktop
- Line height, paragraph spacing, and maximum line length
- Rules for long fortune names and category headings
- Rules for mixed English and Thai text
- Numeral style for fortune numbers

Typography must preserve comfortable reading at the longest English and Thai fortune entries. Do not solve overflow by making the text uncomfortably small.

### Layout deliverables

Provide responsive layouts for at least:

- Small phone: 320px wide
- Typical phone: 390px wide
- Large phone: 430px wide
- Tablet or narrow desktop: 768px wide
- Desktop: 1440px wide

Document:

- Safe areas for notches and browser UI
- Maximum content width
- Bowl size and position at each breakpoint
- Fortune-slip dimensions and aspect ratio
- Text area dimensions and overflow behavior
- Control spacing and minimum touch target sizes
- Logo placement and clear space
- Focus-ring placement
- Portrait share-image composition

Keep the primary action reachable with one hand on a phone. Do not let controls overlap the bowl, crack, slip, or fortune text.

## Fortune Slip Art Direction

Create one shared responsive frame for all 12 fortune records, allowing each record to have supplied art variations within the frame.

The frame must accommodate:

- Fortune number
- Fortune name
- Overall prediction
- Love
- Wealth
- Work and study
- Health
- Optional lucky dish
- Restaurant logo
- English and Thai views

Provide a content-safe template with the longest expected English and Thai samples placed into it. The designer and content owner must review the actual longest entries before handoff.

Decide and document whether each category is:

- Visible on the primary English face
- Revealed through scrolling within the result
- Revealed through an expanded section
- Moved into a secondary fortune view

The result should feel like a fortune slip, but it must remain readable and usable rather than becoming a poster with unreadable text.

## Animation and Motion Deliverables

Provide a storyboard or motion reference for:

1. Ready bowl
2. User activation
3. Wobble and energy build
4. Pressure pause
5. Crack appearance
6. Bowl opening
7. Slip emergence
8. Slip angle correction and settlement
9. Result-ready state
10. Reset for another attempt

For each state, specify:

- Duration
- Easing or physical behavior
- Transform or position changes
- Opacity changes
- Layer order
- Sound cue, if any
- What happens when reduced motion is enabled

Motion requirements:

- Full sequence target: approximately 2-4 seconds
- No animation may block the visitor indefinitely
- Additional taps or shakes do not create duplicate draws during the sequence
- Reduced motion must preserve the meaning and result, not merely hide the animation
- Sound must never be necessary to understand the ritual
- Provide separate art-directed assets for intact, wobble, cracked, and open/release states where needed

## Asset Package

### Required visual assets

- Restaurant logo in color and monochrome variants if approved
- Logo with transparent background
- Fortune bowl artwork for each required state
- Fortune slip frame
- Any per-fortune artwork for all 12 records
- Background or environmental artwork
- Decorative marks, textures, patterns, or food-related motifs
- Loading state artwork
- Failure/retry artwork if part of the art direction
- Social share artwork or templates
- Favicon and app/browser metadata artwork

### Accepted formats

- SVG for scalable illustrations, logos, icons, and line artwork where appropriate
- PNG with transparency for raster artwork, texture, or cases where SVG is not suitable
- Avoid supplying only flattened screenshots for assets that must animate or reflow

For every asset provide:

- Source file
- Optimized web file
- File format
- Pixel dimensions or viewBox
- Intended display size
- Focal point
- Safe crop area
- Transparent-background requirement
- Light/dark or background variants
- Animation state name
- Suggested filename
- Owner and approval status

### Optimization requirements

- Remove unnecessary metadata
- Compress raster images without visible quality loss
- Keep transparent edges clean
- Confirm SVGs render correctly without external linked resources
- Avoid embedding fonts in SVG unless explicitly approved
- Provide a preview sheet showing every state and variant
- Identify any asset that requires a paid license or attribution

## Social Share Image

Design a dedicated portrait composition at `1080 x 1920` pixels.

It must include:

- The currently visible English or Thai fortune
- Fortune number and name where readable
- Restaurant logo
- The selected fortune content only
- No private question
- No personal data
- No analytics or tracking mark

The share composition should reuse the fortune-slip visual identity while allowing enough space for long content. Provide at least one tested longest-content example in both English and Thai.

Keep important content inside a safe area so platform UI does not cover it. The image must remain legible when viewed in a social-media preview, not only at full resolution.

## Handoff Package

Deliver the following:

- Editable source files in the agreed design tool
- Exported SVG and PNG assets
- Responsive screen designs for all required states
- Fortune-slip template
- English and Thai result examples
- Motion storyboard or prototype
- Color tokens and contrast notes
- Typography specification and font licenses
- Spacing, sizing, and breakpoint notes
- Asset inventory with filenames and state mapping
- Social share image at 1080 x 1920
- Favicon and metadata artwork
- Accessibility notes for focus, contrast, reduced motion, and reading order
- A preview sheet of all 12 fortune numbers/names and any per-fortune artwork
- Approval checklist signed off by the restaurant and art-project collaborators

- [ ] Lucky dish can be omitted when not applicable.

- [ ] The crack and slip emergence read as one magical physical moment.
- [ ] Reduced-motion treatment is designed.

- [ ] Focus, error, loading, and retry states are designed.
