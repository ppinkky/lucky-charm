## Workflow

- Backlog: work identified but not ready.
- Ready: requirements and assets are available.
- In progress: actively being worked on.
- Review: awaiting design, restaurant, accessibility, or production verification.
- Done: checked off and accepted.

Close issue 10 only after the production URL, QR code, NFC tag, mobile browsers, desktop browsers, sharing, accessibility, and restaurant conditions have been verified.
